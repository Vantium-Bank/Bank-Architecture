Lifecycle of a Transaction

How Money & Data Move Through the 4 Zones (In Plain English)

End-to-End Latency: < 850 milliseconds
📱 Mobile App Transfer
  1. 1 Customer taps Send Money on Mobile App (Zone 1).
  2. 2 Cloud WAF verifies device identity & encrypts request (Zone 2).
  3. 3 Crosses Direct Connect Highway into Core Middleware (Zone 3).
  4. 4 Finacle Core debits customer account & updates ledger.
  5. 5 Dispatches to NIBSS (Zone 4) to credit recipient bank.
💳 ATM & POS Card Payment
  1. 1 Customer inserts Chip & PIN card at POS terminal (Zone 1).
  2. 2 Reaches on-prem Postilion Switch via private APN (Zone 3).
  3. 3 Passes to Core Middleware for account validation.
  4. 4 Finacle checks balance, holds funds, and approves terminal.
  5. 5 Clears overnight with Interswitch & NIBSS (Zone 4).
🎧 Dispute Resolution
  1. 1 Customer calls or chats with Contact Center (Zone 1).
  2. 2 Agent queries transaction history via Core Middleware.
  3. 3 Directs query to Fiserv Reconciliation (Zone 3).
  4. 4 Instant auto-reversal logged if terminal timed out.
  5. 5 Customer receives automated SMS notification before call ends.
🏛️ Branch Cash & Cheque Clearing
  1. 1 Teller enters deposit into native Finacle UI on branch PC (Zone 1).
  2. 2 Traverses Cisco SD-WAN IPsec tunnel directly to Primary DC.
  3. 3 Direct to Finacle (No Middleware): Finacle validates till limits & sessions.
  4. 4 Oracle RAC commits double-entry ledger debit/credit immediately.
  5. 5 Clearing settled via NIBSS CTS; SMS alert fired via Telco (Zone 4).
🪪 Instant Account & Card Issuance
  1. 1 Customer presents NIN & BVN at Branch KYC Desk (Zone 1).
  2. 2 Secure branch scanner captures live biometrics over SD-WAN.
  3. 3 Inquiry dispatched to NIMC & NIBSS BVN API (Zone 4).
  4. 4 Finacle CIF creates account & generates profile (Zone 3).
  5. 5 Postilion & Matica Printer print & activate Chip card instantly.
